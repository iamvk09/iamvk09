**Authors:** Vibhor Kumar and co-authors

**Publication:** *International Journal of Creative and Open Research in Engineering and Management*, Volume 2, Issue 5, May 2026.

**DOI:** [10.55041/ijcope.v2i5.049](https://doi.org/10.55041/ijcope.v2i5.049)

Research on industrial equipment failure prediction using machine learning and SHAP-based model interpretation. The study explores model evaluation, predictive performance, and ways to make failure predictions more understandable.

**Research Areas:** Explainable AI · LightGBM · Ensemble Learning · Predictive Maintenance · Industrial Analytics

### 02. Local E-Waste Management Audit and Prediction Model: A Machine Learning Approach

**Authors:** Vibhor Kumar, Rahul Shankar Pandey, Omkar Bhowmick

**Journal:** *Journal of Network Security and Data Mining*, Volume 9, Issue 2, pages 14–27.

**Publication DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.17874960](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17874960)

Research on federated machine learning and blockchain for privacy-preserving e-waste forecasting and auditable model updates. The proposed framework allows municipal offices and recycling centers to train locally while recording validated model updates through a permissioned blockchain.

**Research Areas:** Federated Learning · Blockchain · Privacy-Preserving AI · E-Waste Analytics · Auditability
